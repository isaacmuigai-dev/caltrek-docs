Privacy Policy for CalTrek OS

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CalTrek OS ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when gym owners, operators, managers, staff, and their authorized users access our mobile and tablet management application (the "App").

Summary: CalTrek OS is a B2B gym management and Point of Sale (POS) solution. We do not sell your data or your members' data, and we do not display third-party advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account & Authentication Information

When you register or sign in as a gym administrator or staff member, we collect:

  • Personal Contact Information: Your name, business email address, and business phone number.
  • Authentication Credentials: Encrypted credentials managed securely through Firebase Authentication.
  • Facility Details: Gym/facility business name, location branch name, and operational currency settings.

B. Facility Operations & Member Records

To enable the App's core management functionality, gym administrators and staff input and manage customer records:

  • Member Directory: Member names, contact phone numbers, email addresses, emergency contacts, and assigned membership plan tiers.
  • Attendance & Check-Ins: Barcode or manual check-in timestamps, visit counters, and active session status.
  • CRM & Lead Pipeline: Prospective member names, lead status (e.g., walk-in, tour booked, trial active, joined), and follow-up notes.
  • Staff & Shift Records: Staff credentials, role assignments (Admin, Receptionist, Trainer), shift check-ins, and activity logs.

C. Financial & Transaction Records

  • Subscription Billing: Subscriptions for accessing CalTrek OS Pro features are processed directly through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not receive or store your credit card or bank account numbers.
  • Local Point of Sale (POS) Records: In-app transaction logs (membership renewal fees, day passes, personal training sessions, and retail purchases) recorded by staff at the front desk.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Information

We automatically collect diagnostic telemetry to ensure app stability and prevent crashes:

  • Device Attributes: Device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Crash logs and performance diagnostics collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device tokens via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for urgent staff alerts and operational notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for operational and service delivery purposes:

  • To operate the member check-in counter, membership plans, and POS checkout.
  • To maintain your facility's CRM pipeline, lead tracking, and tour scheduling.
  • To provide reliable offline functionality via local device caching (Room DB) with automatic cloud synchronization.
  • To manage multi-branch operational analytics and reporting for head office administration.
  • To diagnose bugs, technical crashes, and improve application responsiveness.

3. Data Storage, Security & Offline-First Architecture

  • Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between the App and our backend servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud data is hosted and managed using Google Cloud Platform and Firebase Firestore with enterprise-grade security and strict access controls.
  • Local Caching: Operational data is cached securely in an encrypted local database (Room DB) on your device to ensure continuous front-desk operations during internet outages.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or monetize your data or your members' data. Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure service providers strictly to deliver the App's services:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: For cloud database hosting, authentication, crash diagnostics, and notifications.
  • Google Play Billing: For secure processing of app subscription plans.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or valid regulatory authority.

5. No Third-Party Advertising

CalTrek OS is funded exclusively through software subscriptions. We do not embed third-party advertising networks (e.g., AdMob), tracker beacons, or behavioral data brokers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access and Updates: Gym administrators can view, edit, or delete any member record, check-in entry, or transaction directly within the App.
  • Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your gym account, all branches, staff credentials, and associated member records at any time.
  • How to Request Deletion: Use the in-app account deletion tool or email us directly at isaacmuigai.dev@gmail.com. Upon verification, all associated cloud records will be permanently purged within 30 days.
