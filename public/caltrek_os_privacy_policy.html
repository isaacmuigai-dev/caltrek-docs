Privacy Policy for FrontAlly OS (CalTrek OS)

Application: FrontAlly OS: Gym POS & CRM (CalTrek Fitness Center Operating System)
Package ID: com.frontallyos.app
Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Summary: FrontAlly OS (CalTrek OS) is a gym management, membership tracking, and Point of Sale (POS) solution. We do not sell user or member data, and we do not display third-party advertisements.

This Privacy Policy describes how FrontAlly OS ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when gym owners, operators, managers, front desk staff, and registered fitness center members access our mobile operating system (the "App").

We process personal data in strict compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect (Google Play Data Safety: 5 Data Types)

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, FrontAlly OS collects and processes 5 specific data types strictly to provide gym operating functionality:

Data Category Specific Elements Operational Purpose Handling & Sharing
1. Personal Info Name, Phone Number, Email Address, Member ID Administrative authentication (Google OAuth), member registration, issuing digital QR check-in tokens, renewal notifications via SMS/WhatsApp. Collected for app functionality; Never sold or shared with advertisers.
2. Photos & Videos Member Profile Portrait Photos Captured via CameraX during member intake to visually verify member identity at the front desk and prevent pass-sharing fraud. Stored securely on Google Cloud Storage with encrypted access.
3. Financial Info M-Pesa Transaction Codes, Cash Amounts, Payment Receipts, Subscription Validity Recording plan subscriptions, settling arrears, reconciling Equity Paybill 247247 transactions, POS retail receipts. Collected for accounting and subscription extension. No credit card numbers or PINs stored.
4. App Info & Performance Crash Logs, UI Rendering Speeds, Network Timings Firebase Crashlytics & Firebase Performance Monitoring to maintain high app stability, identify crashes, and track Firestore latency. Collected automatically as anonymized diagnostic telemetry.
5. Device or Other IDs Firebase App Installation Token, Session Tokens Cryptographic session verification, offline cache synchronization, and push notifications for administrative alerts. Collected for authentication and operational security.

2. Camera & Hardware Permissions

FrontAlly OS requests android.permission.CAMERA for two primary operational tasks:

  • Member Intake Portrait Snap (CameraX): Gym staff capture a member portrait photo during onboarding. The image is uploaded securely to Google Cloud Storage to display on the member dossier card for identity verification.
  • Digital QR Pass & Barcode Scanner: The camera scans member QR passes and barcode badges at the reception desk and turnstiles. Video frames are analyzed locally in volatile memory via Google ML Kit Barcode Vision. Video frames are never saved, streamed, or uploaded.
  • Hardware Declaration: Camera hardware is declared with android:required="false". Facilities without camera hardware can manually check in members by name, phone number, or ID.

3. Financial Records & M-Pesa Integration

  • M-Pesa Paybill & STK Push Reconciliation: The app connects with Safaricom Daraja and Equity Paybill 247247 webhooks to verify payment codes, match paying members by phone, and automatically extend memberships.
  • Ledger Immutability: All payment records stored in Firestore are strictly immutable (enforced by Firestore Security Rules: allow update, delete: if false;) to prevent tampering.
  • Zero Sensitive Financial Data: FrontAlly OS never collects, handles, or stores credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, bank account passwords, or M-Pesa PINs.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising

FrontAlly OS is an enterprise operating tool funded strictly through gym subscriptions. We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob), tracker pixels, or behavioral marketing brokers. The Android Advertising ID is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Data Storage, Security & Offline-First Architecture

  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 cryptographic protection for all network communication between the mobile client and Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Encryption at Rest: AES-256 cloud encryption across all Firestore database documents and Google Cloud Storage buckets.
  • Access Control: Google OAuth authentication with single-owner administrator verification ensures only authorized staff access member dossiers.
  • Local Cache Persistence: Offline Room SQLite caching is stored exclusively in the device's private, sandboxed app directory.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliance)

In adherence to Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion Policy, the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), and GDPR, gym members, staff, and administrators have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

Method A: In-App Immediate Deletion (Self-Service)

Gym administrators and authorized staff can permanently delete any member record directly inside the App:

  1. Open FrontAlly OS on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Member Hub tab.
  3. Select the member's profile card to open the Member Dossier.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the dossier and tap the red "Delete Member Record" button.
  5. Confirm by tapping "Delete Permanently" in the confirmation dialog.
  6. Result: The member profile, phone number, photo, digital QR pass, and any assigned lockers are immediately and permanently erased from the live database.

Method B: Web-Based Data Deletion Request (No App Reinstallation Required)

If you are a member, former staff member, or gym operator who wishes to have your personal data, credentials, or entire gym account permanently deleted without opening the app, you may submit a deletion request online:

Deletion Timelines & Data Handling

  • Personal Data Purged: Member name, phone number, email, portrait photo, digital QR token, fitness assessment notes, and locker assignments are permanently purged within 48 hours of request verification.
  • Authentication Credentials: Associated Firebase Auth credentials and session tokens are revoked and deleted.
  • Financial Records Retention: Transaction codes and cash amounts may be retained in an anonymized format strictly as required by Kenyan commercial tax laws and accounting audit regulations.
